# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## India EV Charging Infrastructure Gap Analysis & Site Recommendation Platform

### 1. Executive Summary
India's electric vehicle (EV) market is experiencing rapid adoption across 2W, 3W, 4W, and commercial bus segments. However, charging station rollout remains unevenly distributed, creating critical "charging deserts" that trigger range anxiety and hamper broad EV adoption. This project provides a spatial analytics and decision intelligence platform to enable government bodies (Ministry of Heavy Industries, NITI Aayog), charge point operators (CPOs), urban planners, and infrastructure investors to prioritize high-impact charging locations.

### 2. Business Objectives
- **Identify Charging Deserts**: Detect regions with high EV registration growth but zero/low public charger density within a 5km radius.
- **Optimize Capital Allocation**: Direct EV charging infrastructure investments to locations with maximum ROI, grid capacity readiness, and highway traffic flow.
- **Support Highway Corridor Readiness**: Assess inter-city charging gaps along major National Highways (NH-48, NH-44, NH-65, etc.).
- **Deliver Explainable Recommendations**: Provide transparent score contribution breakdowns for every recommended site.

### 3. Stakeholders & User Personas
1. **Charge Point Operator (CPO)**: Seeks profitable high-footfall sites with grid access.
2. **State Nodal Officer / Urban Planner**: Seeks equitable charging coverage across urban and rural districts.
3. **Infrastructure Investor**: Evaluates risk-adjusted priority scores before capital deployment.

### 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Charging Accessibility Index**: Average distance to nearest public EV charger.
- **Infrastructure Gap Score**: Ratio of registered EVs to operational public chargers per district.
- **Corridor Gap Coverage**: % of National Highway network with chargers spaced <25km apart.
